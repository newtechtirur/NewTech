PRODUCT IMAGES

Put your product photos in this folder.

The filename must match products.js.
Examples:
student-laptop.jpg
office-laptop.jpg
gaming-laptop.jpg
dome-camera.jpg
keyboard.jpg

If an image is missing, the product card will show its emoji instead.

To add a new product, copy this in products.js:

{
  category: "Laptops",
  icon: "💻",
  name: "HP Laptop 15",
  image: "images/hp-laptop-15.jpg",
  description: "Available in our shop"
},

Then put hp-laptop-15.jpg inside this images folder.
